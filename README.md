# malfoid

> Harry Potter saga fanfic, but Malfoy is a tsundere.

What started as a cursed internet meme somehow became a seven-book alternate-universe rewrite.

The central change is simple: Draco Malfoy becomes **Draconia "Malfoid" Malfoy**, a proud, intelligent, aristocratic Slytherin girl who develops an increasingly inconvenient fixation on Harry Potter.

Everything else is allowed to diverge from there.

## The idea

This is not a word-for-word rewrite of the original books with Draco gender-swapped.

The original Harry Potter saga provides the starting world, major historical events, and broad narrative architecture. Once Malfoid enters the story, however, changes are expected to propagate naturally through the timeline.

Her relationship with Harry develops alongside the conflict with Voldemort rather than replacing it.

The long-term relationship arc is:

**rivals -> friends -> enemies -> lovers**

The goal is a genuine slow burn across all seven years.

Harry and Malfoid begin as competitive eleven-year-olds who find each other deeply irritating. Rivalry gradually becomes fascination, fascination becomes trust, and trust eventually becomes something neither of them is particularly well equipped to admit.

Then the war gets in the way.

By the later books, family loyalty, blood politics, Voldemort, Harry's role in the war, and Malfoid's position inside the Malfoy family force them onto opposite sides of a conflict after they have already become emotionally important to one another.

The final relationship should therefore feel earned rather than inevitable.

## Story principles

A few rules guide the project:

- **No canon railroading.** If an earlier change logically alters a later event, the later event changes.
- **Characters remain recognizable.** Malfoid is not an unrelated original character wearing Draco's name.
- **Malfoid is intelligent.** Her arrogance comes from upbringing, ability, status, and genuine confidence rather than stupidity.
- **She remains a tsundere.** The balance gradually shifts from tsun to dere as she matures.
- **Harry remains Harry.** Brave, stubborn, compassionate, occasionally reckless, and catastrophically slow at recognizing romantic subtext.
- **Hermione notices first.**
- **Ron eventually notices too.**
- **The Voldemort story remains central.** The romance develops inside the larger war.
- **Consequences propagate.** Relationships, knowledge, injuries, secrets, allegiances, and choices must remain consistent across books.

## Structure

The intended emotional progression roughly follows the seven Hogwarts years:

### Year 1 - Rivals

Harry discovers that he matters.

Malfoid begins discovering that being told she matters does not mean everything she has been taught is true.

They immediately annoy each other.

### Years 2-3 - Rivalry becomes friendship

Their obsession with one another becomes increasingly difficult to explain as simple competition. Moments of vulnerability begin slipping through the hostility.

Neither handles this well.

### Years 4-5 - Friendship becomes attachment

Jealousy appears. Trust develops. The political situation becomes increasingly serious, and Malfoid begins confronting the contradictions between her upbringing and the people she actually knows.

Hermione has figured out what is happening.

Harry has not.

### Year 6 - Crisis

Voldemort places enormous pressure on the Malfoy family. Harry knows Malfoid is hiding something and becomes obsessed with discovering what it is.

Their trust collapses.

Sectumsempra should hurt.

A lot.

### Year 7 - Choice

The school rivalry is gone. The war is real.

Harry and Malfoid must decide what they actually believe, who they trust, and what they are willing to sacrifice.

Only after surviving the rupture between them does the slow burn finally become explicit.

## Development

Yes, this fanfic is being treated suspiciously like a software project.

Long-form continuity is difficult, particularly across seven books, so the repository may eventually contain:

- chapter drafts
- character state files
- timelines
- continuity notes
- unresolved plot threads
- divergence tracking
- book outlines

Git exists.

We might as well abuse it.

Example future commit messages:

```text
fix: Hermione knows, Harry remains idiot
feat: add Yule Ball jealousy catastrophe
refactor: remove canon railroading from Chamber arc
feat: destroy relationship with Sectumsempra
BREAKING CHANGE: Malfoid chooses Potter
```

## AI-assisted writing

AI models may be used for drafting, editing, continuity review, brainstorming, and adversarial review.

The intended workflow is closer to an editorial pipeline than "generate seven books in one prompt":

**outline -> draft -> continuity review -> revision -> state update -> next chapter**

The objective is consistency first. Earlier changes must remain meaningful hundreds of pages later.
